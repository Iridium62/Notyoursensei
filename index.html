how it works

What happens on the call

Same three steps every time, whichever path you're on — the order is what makes it work.

01

Recon

We look at what you already have — teaching, service, sales, whatever it is — and figure out what actually transfers. Most people undersell this by a mile.

02

Route

A roadmap that fits your actual budget and hours — certs and projects if you're headed into IT, applications and credentials if it's teaching. You walk away with an order of operations, not a reading list.

03

Execute

I rewrite your resume and application materials so an actual person sees them, not just a filter. Then we drill interviews — the same way I drill for my own exams, over and over until it's automatic.

a quick gut check

Who this is (and isn't) for

THIS IS FOR YOU IF —

  • You've dreamed about living and working in Japan and haven't found your way in yet
  • You're teaching, in hospitality, recruiting, or another field with no clean line to either path
  • You're stuck in a helpdesk or junior IT role and want to actually move up, not just add another certification to the pile
  • You'll actually do the work — you just want the order right
  • You want a plan sized to your week, not someone else's
  • You want a straight answer, even if it's not the one you were hoping for
  • You're willing to put in a few hours a week outside our calls — this only works if you do

THIS ISN'T FOR YOU IF —

  • You want a new job overnight, with no work between now and then
  • You want a resume rewrite with no follow-through on interviews
  • You're already deep into one of these paths — you need execution, not a route
  • You're looking for someone to tell you what you want to hear, not what's true
  • You'd rather talk about making the jump than actually do it
  • You're hoping I know a secret shortcut nobody else has
  • You want someone to do the work for you instead of walking you through it
